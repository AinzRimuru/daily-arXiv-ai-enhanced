### [1] [ClawMobile: Rethinking Smartphone-Native Agentic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22942)
*Hongchao Du,Shangyu Wu,Qiao Li,Riwei Pan,Jinheng Li,Youcheng Sun,Chun Jason Xue*

Main category: cs.MA

TL;DR: 本文推出 ClawMobile，通过分层架构解耦推理与控制，解决了 LLM Agent 在智能手机端运行不稳定的问题，并开源了代码以推动移动端原生智能体的发展。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 智能手机环境具有资源受限、控制接口碎片化和应用状态变化快等挑战，传统的 LLM 难以在移动端实现稳定可靠的自主操作。

Method: 提出了 ClawMobile 框架，采用分层架构（Hierarchical Architecture），将高层语言推理与结构化的确定性控制路径解耦。

Result: 通过 ClawMobile 提升了在真实设备上的执行稳定性与可复现性，并总结了移动端 LLM 运行时（Runtime）的设计原则及在效率、适配性方面的关键挑战。

Conclusion: 构建鲁棒的智能手机原生 Agent 需要在概率性规划（LLM）与确定性系统接口之间建立原则性的协调机制。

Abstract: Smartphones represent a uniquely challenging environment for agentic systems. Unlike cloud or desktop settings, mobile devices combine constrained execution contexts, fragmented control interfaces, and rapidly changing application states. As large language models (LLMs) evolve from conversational assistants to action-oriented agents, achieving reliable smartphone-native autonomy requires rethinking how reasoning and control are composed.
  We introduce ClawMobile as a concrete exploration of this design space. ClawMobile adopts a hierarchical architecture that separates high-level language reasoning from structured, deterministic control pathways, improving execution stability and reproducibility on real devices. Using ClawMobile as a case study, we distill the design principles for mobile LLM runtimes and identify key challenges in efficiency, adaptability, and stability. We argue that building robust smartphone-native agentic systems demands principled coordination between probabilistic planning and deterministic system interfaces. The implementation is open-sourced~\footnote{https://github.com/ClawMobile/ClawMobile} to facilitate future exploration.

### [2] [Social Welfare in Budget Aggregation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23027)
*Javier Cembrano,Rupert Freeman,Ulrike Schmidt-Kraepelin,Markus Utke*

Main category: cs.GT

TL;DR: 本文研究了 $\ell_1$ 效用下的预算聚合问题，提出了 UtilProp 和 GreedyDecomp 等新机制，在保证真实性的同时，优化了比例性与社会福利之间的权衡，并证明了强比例性与最优福利比可以共存。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的公共预算聚合模型在追求福利最大化时可能导致少数群体代表性不足，而现有的比例性概念（如单一比例性）约束力较弱，且比例性与福利、真实性之间的权衡关系尚不明晰。

Method: 1. 引入了兼顾比例性与真实性的新机制 UtilProp；2. 定义了实例级福利支配关系；3. 提出了基于“可分解性”（decomposability）的强比例性机制 GreedyDecomp 和 UtilDecomp；4. 进行了最坏情况比率分析和计算复杂度证明。

Result: 1. UtilProp 实现了最优的最坏情况福利比，且在福利上支配了以往所有同类机制；2. 证明了在不降低福利保证的前提下可实现更强的比例性；3. 证明了计算福利支配的可分解机制（UtilDecomp）是 NP-hard 的，并证明 GreedyDecomp 提供 2-近似保证。

Conclusion: 在比例性约束下，可以在不牺牲最优最坏情况福利比的前提下，实现比传统单一比例性（Single-minded proportionality）更强的比例性概念（如可分解性），且新提出的机制在福利表现上优于现有机制。

Abstract: We study budget aggregation under $\ell_1$-utilities, a model for collective decision making in which agents with heterogeneous preferences must allocate a public budget across a set of alternatives. Each agent reports their preferred allocation, and a mechanism selects an allocation. Early work focused on social welfare maximization, which in this setting admits truthful mechanisms, but may underrepresent minority groups, motivating the study of proportional mechanisms. However, the dominant proportionality notion, single-minded proportionality, is weak, as it only constrains outcomes when agents hold extreme preferences. To better understand proportionality and its interaction with welfare and truthfulness, we address three questions. First, how much welfare must be sacrificed to achieve proportionality? We formalize this via the price of proportionality, the best worst-case welfare ratio between a proportional mechanism and Util, the welfare-maximizing mechanism. We introduce a new single-minded proportional and truthful mechanism, UtilProp, and show that it achieves the optimal worst-case ratio. Second, how do proportional mechanisms compare in terms of welfare? We define an instance-wise welfare dominance relation and use it to compare mechanisms from the literature. In particular, we show that UtilProp welfare-dominates all previously known single-minded proportional and truthful mechanisms. Third, can stronger notions of proportionality be achieved without compromising welfare guarantees? We answer this question in the affirmative by studying decomposability and proposing GreedyDecomp, a decomposable mechanism with optimal worst-case welfare ratio. We further show that computing the welfare-dominant decomposable mechanism, UtilDecomp, is NP-hard, and that GreedyDecomp provides a 2-approximation to UtilDecomp in terms of welfare.

</details>


### [3] [Approximately Solving Continuous-Time Mean Field Games with Finite State Spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23174)
*Yannick Eich,Christian Fabian,Kai Cui,Heinz Koeppl*

Main category: cs.GT

TL;DR: 本文提出了一套针对连续时间离散状态平均场博弈的正则化计算框架，通过扩展固定点迭代和虚构归纳算法，有效解决了大规模多智能体系统的均衡求解难题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 尽管连续时间离散状态平均场博弈（MFG）在理论上很重要，但在计算求解均衡方面仍缺乏高效的方法，现有算法多集中于离散时间模型。

Method: 将正则化方法引入连续时间离散状态MFG，定义了正则化均衡，并将经典的固定点迭代（fixed-point iteration）和虚构归纳法（fictitious play）算法扩展至该模型。

Result: 通过数值实例验证了所提算法在求解连续时间MFG均衡时的有效性和实用性。

Conclusion: 正则化方法不仅在离散时间模型中有效，也成功扩展到了连续时间离散状态场博弈，提高了算法的收敛性和计算效率。

Abstract: Mean field games (MFGs) offer a powerful framework for modeling large-scale multi-agent systems. This paper addresses MFGs formulated in continuous time with discrete state spaces, where agents' dynamics are governed by continuous-time Markov chains -- relevant to applications like population dynamics and queueing networks. While prior research has largely focused on theoretical aspects of continuous-time discrete-state MFGs, efficient computational methods for determining equilibria remain underdeveloped. Inspired by discrete-time approaches, we approximate the classical Nash equilibria by regularization methods, enabling more computationally tractable solution algorithms. Specifically, we define regularized equilibria for continuous-time MFGs and extend the classical fixed-point iteration and fictitious play algorithm to these equilibria. We validate the effectiveness and practicality of our approach via illustrative numerical examples.

### [4] [Impacts of Aggregation on Model Diversity and Consumer Utility](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23293)
*Kate Donahue,Manish Raghavan*

Main category: cs.GT

TL;DR: 本文指出传统胜率指标会导致模型变得雷同，建议采用“加权胜率”来激励AI模型走差异化道路，以构建更健康的模型生态。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在AI模型市场中，用户通过路由选择不同优势的模型来提升整体性能。然而，现有的胜率（Winrate）评价指标可能无法正确引导模型生产者，从而损害市场多样性和消费者效用。

Method: 通过数学建模研究市场进入（新模型加入）和模型替换（现有模型演进）两种情境，并提出了一种基于回答质量权重的“加权胜率”评估机制。

Result: 证明了标准胜率会促使生产者倾向于模仿现有模型（同质化）；而提出的加权胜率机制能有效激励模型在特定任务上专业化，显著提高了存在选择噪声时的系统稳健性。

Conclusion: 现有的胜率指标会激励模型同质化，而加权胜率能够从理论和实证上引导模型专业化，从而提升消费者福利。

Abstract: Consider a marketplace of AI tools, each with slightly different strengths and weaknesses. By selecting the right model for the task at hand, a user can do better than simply committing to a single model for everything. Routers operate under a similar principle, where sophisticated model selection can increase overall performance. However, aggregation is often noisy, reflecting in imperfect user choices or routing decisions. This leads to two main questions: first, what does a "healthy marketplace" of models look like for maximizing consumer utility? Secondly, how can we incentivize producers to create such models? Here, we study two types of model changes: market entry (where an entirely new model is created and added to the set of available models), and model replacement (where an existing model has its strengths and weaknesses changed). We show that winrate, a standard benchmark in LLM evaluation, can incentivize model creators to homogenize for both types of model changes, reducing consumer welfare. We propose a new mechanism, weighted winrate, which rewards models for answers that are higher quality, and show that it provably improves incentives for producers to specialize and increases consumer welfare. We conclude by demonstrating that our theoretical results generalize to empirical benchmark datasets and discussing implications for evaluation design.

</details>

### [5] [Multi-modal Data Driven Virtual Base Station Construction for Massive MIMO Beam Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22796)
*Yijie Bian,Wei Guo,Jie Yang,Shenghui Song,Jun Zhang,Shi Jin,Khaled B. Letaief*

Main category: cs.IT

TL;DR: 本文提出一种基于 3D LiDAR 辅助的可解释框架，通过构建虚拟基站（VBS）辅助波束对准。该方法在混合信道环境下显著降低了开销，并实现了接近最优的频谱效率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Massive MIMO 是 6G 的关键技术，但其性能受限于高训练开销带来的波束管理难题，特别是在复杂的 LoS（视距）和 NLoS（非视距）混合传播环境中。

Method: 提出一种可解释的框架：利用多模态数据（3D LiDAR）重建反射面，并据此构建“虚拟基站（VBS）”，即基站相对于反射面的镜像点。基于 VBS 进行粗略信道重建，随后进行局部波束训练。

Result: 数值仿真结果表明，该方法在频谱效率方面达到了接近最优的性能。

Conclusion: 该框架有效地解决了混合传播环境下的波束管理问题。通过将几何物理特性融入设计，不仅提高了频谱效率，还降低了传统方法的开销，为 6G 高频通信提供了极具潜力的技术方案。

Abstract: Massive multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) is a key enabler for the high data rates required by the sixth-generation networks, yet its performance hinges on effective beam management with low training overhead. This paper proposes an interpretable framework to tackle beam alignment in mixed line-of-sight (LoS) and non-line-of-sight (NLoS) propagation environments. Our approach utilizes multi-modal data to construct virtual base stations (VBSs), which are geometrically defined as mirror images of the base station across reflecting surfaces reconstructed from 3D LiDAR points. These VBSs provide a sparse and spatial representation of the dominant features of the wireless environment. Based on the constructed VBSs, we develop a VBS-assisted beam alignment scheme comprising coarse channel reconstruction followed by partial beam training. Numerical results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves near-optimal performance in terms of spectral efficiency.

### [6] [Secure Transmission for Fluid Antenna-Aided ISAC Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23241)
*Yunxiao Li,Qian Zhang,Xuejun Cheng,Zhiguo Wang,Xiaoyan Wang,Hongji Xu,Ju Liu*

Main category: cs.IT

TL;DR: 本文研究了基于液体天线（FA）的ISAC系统安全传输，通过联合优化天线位置与波束成形，在存在感知目标窃听的情况下，实现了秘密速率的大幅提升。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有ISAC系统研究尚未解决当感知目标同时作为窃听者时，如何通过液体天线（FA）的灵活性最大化多用户总秘密速率的问题。

Method: 采用块相继上界最小化（BSUM）算法，结合近端距离算法（PDA）更新波束成形矢量，以及外推投影梯度法（EPG）优化天线位置向量。

Result: 仿真结果表明，所提出的FA-ISAC方案相比固定位置天线（FPA）系统，其总秘密速率提升了20%以上。

Conclusion: 在ISAC系统中使用液体天线（FA）能显著提升物理层安全性能，尤其是面对潜在的窃听目标时。

Abstract: Fluid antenna (FA) has become a highly promising technology and has recently been used to enhance the integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) system. However, the scenario where sensing targets act as eavesdroppers in ISAC and how to maximize the sum secrecy rate has not been addressed. This letter investigates secure transmission in FA-aided ISAC systems, where the spatial agility of FAs enables enhanced physical layer security. We jointly optimize antenna position vector (APV) and beamforming to maximize the multiuser sum secrecy rate, which complicates the solution process. To solve the resulting non-convex problem, we use a block successive upper-bound minimization (BSUM) algorithm, which incorporates the proximal distance algorithm (PDA) for closed-form beamformer updates and extrapolated projected gradient (EPG) for APV optimization. Simulation results show that the proposed FA-ISAC scheme achieves over 20$\%$ sum secrecy rate gain compared to fixed-position antenna (FPA) systems.

</details>
